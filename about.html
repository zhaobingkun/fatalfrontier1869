An unofficial field guide with visible sources.

Fatal Frontier 1869 Wiki exists to answer practical player questions without pretending developer marketing, community theory and verified game data are the same thing.

Independence: this site is not operated, sponsored or endorsed by Paydirt Games. Fatal Frontier 1869 names and game material belong to their respective owners. Original illustrations on this Wiki are unofficial editorial artwork, not screenshots or game assets.

What is the site for?

The Wiki organizes information about downloading the game, starting an expedition, choosing Pioneers, understanding equipment and Traits, completing quests, prospecting, extracting and handling the optional real-value systems. It is built as a static English reference so important answers remain visible without JavaScript.

The project entered early because the game is new and third-party documentation is limited. That makes careful sourcing more important, not less.

Which sources do we trust?

SourceHow it is used
Official Terms and policiesHighest priority for eligibility, payments, ownership, refunds and account rules.
Official FAQ and patch notesPrimary source for intended mechanics, modes, characters and changes.

Independent reviewsUseful for tested play experience, controls and criticism.
Community reportsAnecdotal evidence, labeled clearly and never converted into a universal result.

How do we cover money?

The site does not promise earnings, recommend chasing losses or publish invented payout rates. A withdrawal route is described only when linked to current rules. Developer-reported totals remain attributed. Player payout stories are presented as individual reports, not average returns.

Strategy pages distinguish Gold Grains, Greenbacks, Claims, local resources and completed U.S. dollar withdrawals. Readers should use the free survival game first and spend only what they can afford to lose.

How are unknowns handled?

Unverified damage, drop rates, map positions, quest triggers and prices are marked as field data or test needed. A blank is preferable to a confident invention. When community theory conflicts with an official technical description, both can be described with their source and limitation.

How are pages updated?

Every evidence-sensitive page carries a checked or modified date. Early Access updates are reviewed first for effects on beginner routes, economy, Pioneers, equipment and extraction. Old numeric data should not survive a patch without re-verification.

Corrections and attribution

If a source changes, a mechanic is misdescribed or an illustration could be mistaken for official art, the correction should update the page, its structured data, internal links and sitemap date together. External passages are summarized and linked rather than reproduced at length.

This project currently publishes no user-submission form. Corrections can be compared against the official sources linked on each article; a public contact route should be added when the production domain and mailbox are configured.

Privacy and external links

The site uses Google Analytics 4 for aggregate usage measurement, but it does not contain advertising, account creation or payment collection. External download, support and community links leave this Wiki and follow the destination’s policies. See the Privacy page for details.
