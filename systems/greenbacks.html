Greenbacks buy options, not withdrawals.

Greenbacks are an in-game supply currency used across expeditions and shops. Keep them separate from local expedition money, Claims, Gold Grains and actual U.S. dollars.

Direct answer: Greenbacks are an account-level in-game currency. Current official material says they can buy gear, special items, ammunition and rations, and can be earned through certain quests or selling specific items. They are not directly withdrawable U.S. dollars.

How do you earn Greenbacks?

The August 19 patch introduced “Greenback Earn”: Pioneers can receive Greenbacks from quest rewards and by selling specific items at shops. The patch does not say every quest or every item qualifies. Verify the reward or sale value in the current interface.

  • Complete quests that explicitly list Greenbacks.
  • Sell items the current shop marks as eligible.
  • Receive a live starter or event allocation when offered.
  • Purchase Greenbacks through the current Game Lobby or shop.
  • Exchange Gold Grains for Greenbacks at the Bank in Town under the current patch.

Promotions and exchange terms can change. Do not treat an advertised starter value as cash received.

What can Greenbacks buy?

The official FAQ broadly says gear and special items. August patch notes specifically mention scarce ammunition and rations as items available for Greenbacks. An independent hands-on review also reports equipment, tickets, Pioneer-related options and recovery uses, but those details should be checked against the live build before purchase.

What should you buy first?

Spend to solve the failure that ends your expedition. If ammunition ends the run, reserve ammunition. If energy and food block the return, prioritize rations. If the current tool can no longer complete the objective, compare replacement value with the route’s remaining reward.

  1. Reserve enough for the exit route.
  2. Buy survival essentials before convenience.
  3. Match tools to the intended resource.
  4. Avoid upgrading every slot at once.
  5. Keep a small emergency balance.
  6. Record the price because Early Access balance changes.

Should you exchange Gold Grains for Greenbacks?

The August patch allows Gold Grains to purchase Claims and Greenbacks at the Bank. That creates a choice between keeping an eligible real-value balance and converting it into more in-game activity. Compare the exact exchange rate and intended use before confirming.

Decision rule: exchange only when the extra in-game utility matters more to you than preserving the current Gold balance. Conversion is not automatically profitable.

What does the starter allocation mean?

The FAQ currently advertises 1,500 Greenbacks as part of a broader new-player package. That quantity is developer-valued within the bundle. It is not 1,500 U.S. dollars and is not proof of a permanent offer. Confirm the current account balance and activation terms in the Lobby.

Frequently asked questions

Are Greenbacks real money?

No. They are an in-game currency with no direct withdrawal route published.

Can you earn Greenbacks without paying?

Yes. Current patch notes list quest rewards and selling specific items at shops.

Do Greenbacks carry between expeditions?

The August Shop UI distinguishes in-hand and account-level currencies; Greenbacks are presented as account-level. Verify the current balance before and after a run.

Can Greenbacks buy Claims?

The official patch says Gold Grains can buy Claims and Greenbacks at the Bank. It does not clearly say Greenbacks themselves buy Claims, so do not assume that exchange direction.
