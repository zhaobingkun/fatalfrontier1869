Contact the independent Wiki.

Use the public project tracker for corrections, broken links, attribution questions and evidence that can improve a guide.

Editorial contact: open a public issue in the Wiki’s GitHub repository. Do not post account credentials, payment information, identity documents, private support tickets or personal balances.

What should be reported here?

  • An official rule, character stat or Early Access mechanic has changed.
  • A source link, internal link, image or video no longer works.
  • A page presents a player report too confidently or misses important context.
  • An illustration, quotation or attribution needs correction.
  • You have reproducible, privacy-safe evidence that answers a field-data question.

Open an editorial issue ↗

What makes a useful correction?

Include the affected page URL, a short description of the problem, the current game version or observation date, and a public source when one exists. Screenshots should hide email addresses, account identifiers, balances, identity data and payment details. A correction can be useful without revealing who you are.

What does not belong here?

This Wiki cannot recover accounts, change eligibility, investigate a withdrawal, accept KYC documents or troubleshoot private payment records. Those requests belong with the official Fatal Frontier Support page. Never publish a launcher token, government ID or payment credential in a GitHub issue.

Why use a public tracker?

A public issue creates a visible correction trail and lets a reader see whether a claim was questioned, sourced and resolved. The repository belongs to this independent Wiki project; it is not operated by Paydirt Games.
