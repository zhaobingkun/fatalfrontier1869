Justice: Wyatt Earp’s close-work shotgun.

The name, owner and weapon type are official. Damage, spread, reload speed and acquisition details still belong in the field notebook.

Short answer: Justice is the legendary double-barreled shotgun named in Wyatt Earp’s official Pioneer profile. The description frames it as a close-range weapon, while Wyatt’s +15% Ranged Damage supports the pairing.

Which Justice stats are unknown?

The public profile does not publish base damage, pellet count, spread, effective range, ammunition type, reload duration, durability, repair cost or whether Justice is permanently bound to Wyatt. Those values must be taken from the current game build before this page becomes a numerical weapon guide.

Why does it fit Wyatt?

Wyatt’s published Health, Energy and Encumbrance are all 120, giving him more margin than most current Pioneers. His ranged-damage bonus supports firearms, while additional daytime view range helps identify danger before it closes to shotgun distance.

This does not make Justice automatically best in slot. Ammunition cost, reload exposure, enemy armor, carrying weight and future balance changes could all change the practical answer.

Practical field plan

  1. Use Wyatt’s sight advantage to choose the engagement before closing distance.
  2. Keep a retreat lane open; a double-barreled weapon may create a vulnerable reload window.
  3. Reserve pack space for ammunition and recovery rather than treating high Encumbrance as permission to carry everything.
  4. Capture the item tooltip and current build number before recording any numerical stat.

Field note

The close-range advice follows the official description, but the reload warning is a cautious planning principle—not a confirmed Justice timing value.
