Nellie Cashman: utility, energy and a loyal Husky.

Nellie spreads her bonuses across ranged damage, chopping, mining and defense, while her 120 Energy supports longer working routes.

Best for: players who want one Pioneer to contribute across several field jobs. Nellie has no single extreme gathering bonus, but her Energy and companion make her roster role distinct.

Who is Nellie Cashman?

The official profile portrays Nellie as an Irish healer, prospector and survivor accompanied by Finnegan, a Husky. It states that animal companions assist Pioneers in surviving the frontier, but does not publish Finnegan’s exact combat, detection or carrying values.

Nellie Cashman stats and abilities

Abilities+10% Ranged Damage, +10% Chopping Speed, +1 Defense, +10% Mining Speed
Health90
Energy120
Encumbrance90
CompanionFinnegan, Husky

Nellie’s 120 Energy is the strongest base number in her profile. Encumbrance remains 90, so longer activity does not automatically mean a larger safe haul.

Practical build direction

Use Nellie as a flexible field worker: carry a ranged weapon, one primary gathering tool and enough recovery to exploit her Energy. Choose the expedition’s main job before leaving rather than packing for mining and chopping at full depth on the same run.

Field note

Finnegan is a confirmed animal companion, but this Wiki will not invent pet damage, pathing or passive bonuses before they are verified in game.
