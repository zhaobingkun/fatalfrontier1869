Is Fatal Frontier 1869 on Steam?

No official Steam store page appeared in our August 27, 2026 check. More importantly, current first-party material consistently sends players to the Paydirt Launcher, and Paydirt describes the release as independent of third-party app stores.

Do not confuse Steam with the Steamer

The game includes a Steamer extraction route, but that is an in-game ship—not Valve’s Steam store. Search results can mix the two words.

This status can change. If the developer announces a Steam version later, the safest confirmation will be a link from FatalFrontier.com to a verified Steam store page.
