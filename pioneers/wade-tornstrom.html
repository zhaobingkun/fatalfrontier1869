Wade Tornstrom: the free starting Pioneer.

Wade is a former whaling captain with melee damage, defense and daytime sight bonuses. His low Energy makes route discipline more important than his imposing appearance suggests.

Best for: learning the basic expedition loop without buying another Pioneer. Wade favors direct melee encounters and daytime scouting, but his 60 Energy gives long or inefficient routes less room for error.

Who is Wade Tornstrom?

Wade is the Pioneer every player receives for free. The official profile describes him as a shipwrecked whaling captain and harpooner who now treats a pickaxe like another tool for opening a stubborn beast. That background points toward a physical, straightforward play style rather than stealth or ranged specialization.

The melee and defense bonuses make nearby threats more manageable, while added daytime view range helps you read the route before committing. Energy is the clear constraint: Wade should not be treated as an unlimited-distance hauler simply because his story emphasizes strength and endurance.

Practical build direction

Start with survivability, energy efficiency and a dependable melee weapon. Use short loops between a camp, gathering point and known exit until you understand how much movement and work Wade can sustain. Traits that reduce route friction are likely more useful early than stacking damage alone.

Field note

This is a route recommendation based on Wade’s published bonuses, not a confirmed best-in-slot build. Trait availability and balance can change during Early Access.

Practical expedition use

Use Wade when the route asks for a steady generalist who can carry a sensible load and survive contact. His published defensive and melee direction is useful for a beginner learning when to disengage, but the lower Energy value means a heavy pack and long sprint chain can still create a bad return. Test a small loadout first and treat his free availability as a reason to learn the loop, not proof that he is best at every job.

Baseline rule: keep the route and supplies fixed before comparing Wade with another Pioneer. A different result does not establish hidden damage, drop-rate or payout effects.

When is Wade the right choice?

Choose Wade when you are learning the basic expedition rhythm and want to test systems without judging a paid Pioneer at the same time. His modest melee, defense and daylight-view bonuses support short routes where the main goal is to understand movement, gathering, combat and extraction. Because every player receives him, he is also the cleanest baseline for comparing how another Pioneer changes a run.

Do not choose Wade for a route that depends on exceptional Energy, carrying capacity or a specialized gathering bonus. Nellie publishes twice Wade’s Energy, while Russell and Boomstick Bob list 120 Encumbrance. Those profiles may better fit long work loops or tool-heavy loads, but they also change more than one variable. Compare one route with the same supplies before deciding that a roster upgrade caused the result.

  • Good test: repeat a short daylight route with a fixed pack.
  • Watch closely: remaining Energy before the return leg.
  • Do not infer: that free availability means weak performance or that a paid Pioneer guarantees a better outcome.
