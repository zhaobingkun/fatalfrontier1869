Russell Greeneberry: mine quickly, carry the result.

Russell combines mining speed with 120 Encumbrance and a broad set of combat, movement and stealth bonuses.

Best for: resource-focused expeditions that need room for tools and a larger haul. Russell’s published kit is broad rather than extreme, giving him useful answers across mining, movement and survival.

Who is Russell Greeneberry?

The official profile describes Russell as a Colorado prospector who can recognize paydirt and work bedrock quickly. His custom pack supports the practical part of that fantasy: 120 Encumbrance gives him one of the largest listed carrying capacities in the current roster.

Russell Greeneberry stats and abilities

Abilities+10% Mining Speed, +2 Sprint Speed, +10% Melee Damage, +2 Defense, -1 Sleep Hour, +5% Dodge, +5% Stealth
Health85
Energy90
Encumbrance120

Russell can carry more than Clara, Wade or Nellie, but his 85 Health does not make him a front-line tank. The stealth and dodge bonuses are valuable because avoiding a costly encounter protects the haul he is built to collect.

Practical build direction

Use Russell for planned mining loops with a known extraction route. Favor mining efficiency, pack management and enough melee reliability to survive an interrupted dig. Extra capacity should hold profitable supplies and loot, not encourage an expedition to stay out after its safe exit window has closed.

Field note

A large pack increases opportunity and risk at the same time. Decide what you will abandon before the route becomes an emergency.
