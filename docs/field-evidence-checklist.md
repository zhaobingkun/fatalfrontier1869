# Field evidence collection protocol

Last reviewed: 2026-09-15.

## What this release does and does not prove

The current batch contains source-checked mechanics, editorial checklists, and original SVG explanatory diagrams. It is not a client playtest, weapon benchmark, or withdrawal test. No real launcher screenshot or payment receipt was captured.

## Safe capture record

For every observation record: client build/version if shown, capture date/time/time zone, mode, Pioneer and equipped traits, action immediately before the observation, exact visible result, and source file. Unknown fields remain unknown.

## Launcher

Capture installer, client start, download/update and login/service failures separately. Keep the full message and stage. For stuck progress capture two observation times. Redact personal details, credentials, authentication tokens and payment/ID information before publication. Confirm permission to publish user-submitted screenshots.

## Beginner sequence

Record tutorial movement, found-weapon equip, visible gathering, camping/recovery, trading and one complete expedition exit. Show before/after states and distinguish Save from extraction. Do not label a recommended sequence as a recorded run.

## Extraction

Capture the dock, ticket requirement, actual ship availability, Signal Fire interaction and Lobby result. Do not add coordinates, resource requirements, enemy counts or wait durations before observing them in the current build. A procedural expedition does not establish a universal route.

## Equipment comparison

Capture same-build tooltips and keep the Pioneer, traits, target type, condition and distance consistent. Repeat an action, record each outcome and separate tooltip facts from measured results and hypotheses. No unmeasured damage/reload/weight/durability/earnings values or best-in-slot claims.

## Withdrawal

Only an eligible account holder may test a withdrawal. Do not make purchases, send identity documents, mask location or submit payments as part of editorial maintenance. Public evidence must redact account and processor identifiers. Distinguish extracted balance, request confirmation, processor status and destination receipt; a request is not a receipt or proof of profit.
