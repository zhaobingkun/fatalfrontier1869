Which combat roles are confirmed?

The current Pioneer profiles publish bonuses to ranged damage, melee damage, defense, dodge, movement and sight. Those bonuses confirm that weapon choice interacts with a Pioneer’s role, but they do not reveal an exact weapon tier list.

Combat directionPublished evidenceUseful starting point
RangedWyatt, Clara, Jane and Nellie publish ranged-damage bonuses.Clara favors distance; Wyatt has the named shotgun.
MeleeWade and Skookum Joe publish melee-damage bonuses.Use a dependable melee option while preserving energy and an exit route.
DemolitionBoomstick Bob’s official profile describes a demolitionist role.Exact explosive items and values remain unverified.

How should you choose a weapon now?

Start with the Pioneer’s published bonuses, the route’s likely enemy pressure and the weight you can afford. A weapon that matches a damage bonus can still be a poor expedition choice if ammunition or carry pressure leaves no room for recovery tools and loot.

Wiki rule

A weapon receives its own page only when we have a confirmed name, a reliable source and enough useful detail to answer a player question. Unknown fields stay visible as unknown.
