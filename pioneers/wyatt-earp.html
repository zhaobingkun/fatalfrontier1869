Wyatt Earp: steady pressure at close range.

Wyatt combines sturdy base stats, ranged damage and extra daytime sight with Justice, his named double-barreled shotgun.

Best for: players who want a steady all-round stat line and decisive ranged combat. Wyatt’s published 100 Health, 110 Energy and 100 Encumbrance give him useful margin without making him a pure hauler.

Who is Wyatt Earp?

The official profile presents Wyatt as a lawman, gambler and cold-eyed survivor. His signature weapon is Justice, a double-barreled shotgun built for close work. The profile does not publish weapon damage, reload speed or ammunition values, so those details should be confirmed in game before comparing it with other firearms.

Wyatt’s numbers support deliberate combat routes without turning him into the roster’s largest hauler. The ranged bonus is broadly useful, while daytime sight helps spot threats before they become shotgun-distance problems.

Practical build direction

Favor ammunition discipline, ranged reliability and enough recovery to preserve Wyatt’s balanced stat advantage. His profile suggests close-range pressure, but the safer route is still to identify threats early and decide whether the encounter is worth the cost. Avoid assuming that a named weapon makes every fight profitable.

Field note

Wyatt currently looks like a steady combat generalist on published stats. That is not the same as a universal tier-list verdict; acquisition cost, trait access and future balance also matter.

Practical expedition use

Use Wyatt when a close-range answer and a clear sightline matter more than quiet resource farming. Justice is the named double-barreled shotgun in the official profile, while his ranged, defense and view bonuses suggest a deliberate combat role. The profile does not publish damage, ammo or reload data, so plan around positioning and extraction rather than assuming the signature weapon solves every encounter.

Combat rule: record the weapon, ammunition used and extraction result together. Wyatt’s published ranged bonus does not supply the missing Justice damage or reload values.

Wyatt or Clara for a ranged route?

Wyatt is the steadier published profile: 100 Health, 110 Energy and 100 Encumbrance leave more room for supplies and mistakes. Clara offers stronger movement, sprint and stealth direction but lists only 75 Health and 70 Encumbrance. Choose Wyatt when the route may force a fight or when you need enough capacity for ammunition, recovery and loot. Choose Clara when avoiding contact and repositioning are the plan.

The comparison does not establish a damage winner. Both publish +15% Ranged Damage, while the available public material does not provide a complete weapon catalog or a repeatable Justice damage test. Use the same route, ammunition budget and extraction point if you want a useful comparison; changing the Pioneer, weapon and route at once produces a story, not evidence.

  • Wyatt advantage: more forgiving base stats and daylight view.
  • Clara advantage: movement, sprint, dodge and stealth direction.
  • Still unknown: exact weapon damage, reload exposure and acquisition cost.
