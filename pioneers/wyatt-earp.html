Wyatt Earp: steady pressure at close range.

Wyatt combines high base stats, ranged damage and extra daytime sight with Justice, his named double-barreled shotgun.

Best for: players who want a forgiving all-round stat line and decisive ranged combat. Wyatt’s published 120 Health, Energy and Encumbrance give him more baseline margin than most of the current roster.

Who is Wyatt Earp?

The official profile presents Wyatt as a lawman, gambler and cold-eyed survivor. His signature weapon is Justice, a double-barreled shotgun built for close work. The profile does not publish weapon damage, reload speed or ammunition values, so those details should be confirmed in game before comparing it with other firearms.

Wyatt’s numbers support longer routes, larger hauls and a bigger safety buffer. The ranged bonus is broadly useful, while daytime sight helps spot threats before they become shotgun-distance problems.

Practical build direction

Favor ammunition discipline, ranged reliability and enough recovery to preserve Wyatt’s large stat advantage. His profile suggests close-range pressure, but the safer route is still to identify threats early and decide whether the encounter is worth the cost. Avoid assuming that a named weapon makes every fight profitable.

Field note

Wyatt currently looks like a strong generalist on published stats. That is not the same as a universal tier-list verdict; acquisition cost, trait access and future balance also matter.

Practical expedition use

Use Wyatt when a close-range answer and a clear sightline matter more than quiet resource farming. Justice is the named double-barreled shotgun in the official profile, while his ranged, defense and view bonuses suggest a deliberate combat role. The profile does not publish damage, ammo or reload data, so plan around positioning and extraction rather than assuming the signature weapon solves every encounter.

Build rule: match the published role to the route, then test the smallest loadout that can survive and extract. Unknown damage, drop rates, durability and payout effects stay unconfirmed.
