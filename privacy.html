Privacy in the current static build.

This page explains the Wiki's current Vercel and Cloudflare hosting, Google Analytics measurement, browser storage and external links.

Current status: the site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate visits and page usage. It has no advertising network, account system, comments, payment form or identity-document upload, and it does not ask for game passwords, KYC documents or payment details.

Basic hosting records

The site is hosted on Vercel and currently uses Cloudflare for DNS and proxy delivery. These providers may process standard technical records such as IP address, requested URL, browser information, timestamps and security events under their own policies.

Browser storage

The site uses Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-KMLT9384LR. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser storage and may process page URLs, referrers, browser and device details, approximate location and interaction events. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Analytics opt-out tool.

External links

Links to FatalFrontier.com, Paydirt Games, Reddit, independent reviews, Discord or payment-related source pages leave this Wiki. Those sites can collect data under their own privacy policies. An external link is a source or route, not an endorsement.

Embedded video

Selected guide pages embed YouTube videos using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. The iframe is lazy-loaded, but opening or playing it can still send technical data to Google or YouTube under their policies. Each video is also available as a direct external link.

Sensitive information

Do not send this Wiki passwords, launcher tokens, payment details, Social Security information or identity documents. The site has no feature that requires them. Official account, KYC, payment and withdrawal questions belong with Paydirt Games or its named processors.

Age and prospecting

This Wiki is informational. Paydirt’s current Terms contain separate age, identity and location requirements for the game and Prospecting Mode. Readers should use the official Terms rather than infer eligibility from access to a guide.

Changes to this policy

The page will be revised when the hosting, analytics configuration, advertising, consent controls or contact method changes. The modified date moves with material implementation changes.

Questions

For this Wiki’s corrections, attribution or privacy questions, use the public Contact page. Do not include account credentials, payment records, balances or identity documents. For game accounts, payments or technical support, use the official Fatal Frontier Support page.

Last updated September 26, 2026. This policy reflects the current Vercel deployment, Cloudflare delivery and Google Analytics 4 configuration.
